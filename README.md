## Why you’ll want to play

Most colony games ask you to **conquer** the wild.

**Wilderfolk** asks you to **move into it** — and live with the consequences.

Your people fall in love, argue at work, sneak out at night, and trade lines in speech bubbles.  
Deer eat the grass. Wolves eat the deer. Your hunters want the same trails.  
Caravans camp at the edge of the map. Rivals claim the next ridge. Winter burns your woodpile.

Wipe the wolves? You’ll feel clever for a season.  
Then the deer explode, the meadows die, and the hunting packs come home empty.

**You didn’t lose to a raid. You lost to ecology.**

```
  🌿 grass  →  🐰 🦌 prey  →  🐺 🦊 predators  →  🏹 you  →  🏘️ village
```

That loop is the heart of the game. Farms, walls, taverns, elections, and trade are how you stay **human** inside it.

---

## The fantasy

You’re the founder of a **frontier village**. Two pioneers. Empty land. Night coming.

You place a house before dark. A farm at dawn. People walk to work, home, the tavern.  
Children grow up. Couples marry. Affairs leave scars. Orphans get adopted.  
Visitors stroll into the market, pray at church, or put on a show in the square.  
Full moons turn cursed kin into **Moon Howlers**. Rivals draw red raid lines across the map.  
Long trade routes need a **Market**. Harmony needs **wild** wolves still free.

It’s a **colony sim** with a **food chain**, **families**, and **drama** — not a spreadsheet with trees.

## What you can actually do

A full menu of systems — so you know what’s in the game before you boot it.

### Build a real village

Place buildings from the **left build catalog** (or **B**). Incomplete sites get **builders**; finished job buildings get **workers** (auto-staff or click **+ Assign**).

| Category | Buildings | What they’re for |
|----------|-----------|------------------|
| **Homes** | House, Mansion | Sleep, families, immigration capacity; upgrade houses for more beds |
| **Food** | Farm, Greenhouse, Barn, Silo, Mill, Hunting Spot | Grow food, store it, process it, hunt wildlife (wolves may fight back) |
| **Industry** | Lumber Mill, Quarry, Mine, Blacksmith, Workshop | Wood, stone, forge iron gear, craft goods for gold |
| **Trade** | Store, Market | Gold income; **Market unlocks long-range trade routes** |
| **Civic** | Town Hall, School, Hospital, Church, Well, Prison | Taxes/festivals/elections, children grow faster, health, morals & Moon Howler cures, water, scandal sentences |
| **Life** | Tavern, Taming Post | Evening hangouts; tame wildlife (Harmony victory still needs **untamed** wolves) |
| **Defense** | Wall / Corner / Gate, Watchtower, Barracks, Road | Barricade strength, raid warning, guards on patrol, faster travel (roads cut habitat) |

Drag **walls and roads** in strips; **R** rotates. Watch construction % climb day by day.

### Run the economy

- **Resources:** wood 🪵 · stone 🪨 · food 🍖 · gold 🪙 (header chips)  
- **Storage caps** grow with barns, silos, stores, markets  
- **Food spoils** (silos help); winter is hungrier  
- **Workshop recipes** — wooden goods, tools, furniture, trinkets  
- **Blacksmith forge queue** — iron spears, shields, guard gear, wall plates, pickaxes (research + staffed smith, multi-day jobs)  
- **Trade routes** (Progress → Trade) — after reputation **and** a Market; merchants walk caravans on the map  

### Live a day with your people

One in-game day = **24 hours** on the clock.

| When | What settlers do |
|------|------------------|
| **Night (8pm–6am)** | Home if they have a house; map darkens, windows glow |
| **Workdays Mon–Fri 7am–6pm** | Commute to farm, mill, smith, school, barracks… |
| **Evenings** | Some stay in; some go out (tavern, stroll, socialize) |
| **Weekends** | Free days — lazy or active, person by person |

**Families:** courtship 💕, marriage 💍, pregnancy, children, surnames, move-outs at 18+, orphan adoption, shared houses when beds are tight.  
**Chat:** speech bubbles from a big dialogue bank (work, home, fear, festivals, visitors, winter…).  
**Jobs:** farmer, lumberjack, miner, smith, merchant, teacher, doctor, priest, guard, hunter, innkeeper…  
**Scandals:** affairs, prison, reputation hits.  
**School:** staffed school → kids attend by day, grow up a bit faster.

### Shape the land & seasons

| Choice | What it does |
|--------|----------------|
| **Map size** | Small · Medium · Large |
| **Land type** | Verdant · Mountainous · Coastal · Arid · Harsh |
| **Seasons** | Spring boom → Summer → Fall harvest → **Winter** (day ~270): burn wood to heat, grass nearly stops |
| **Weather** | Clear, rain, snow, storm, fog, drought — storms hurt buildings; rain helps grass |
| **Header climate** | Season + daily temperature feel |

**Ecosystem health** (Nature tab) is your second health bar. Pollution rises if you pave everything.

### Wildlife & the food chain

| Who | Role |
|-----|------|
| **Grass & trees** | Base of the valley; lumber mills care about nearby trees |
| **Rabbits** | Fast breeders, prey for foxes/wolves/you |
| **Deer** | Big grazers — too many = bare meadows |
| **Foxes** | Rabbit control |
| **Wolves** | Apex pressure on deer (and sometimes your hunters) |
| **Wildkin** | Rare gentle wanderers |

**You can hunt** (settlers chase prey — orange dashed lines on the map).  
**Predators hunt** too (grey lines). Over-hunting or wiping wolves breaks the chain.

### Visitors (they walk into town)

Cyan camps near your village. Seven kinds:

| Kind | They tend to… |
|------|----------------|
| **Traders** | Hang around Market / Store |
| **Pilgrims** | Church, quiet prayer |
| **Scholars** | School / Town Hall |
| **Hunters** | Trails & hunting spots |
| **Nomads** | Tavern, wells, homes |
| **Refugees** | Beg Town Hall / Hospital — **you** choose welcome / screen / turn away |
| **Performers** | Square / tavern — shows, revelry, courtship sparks |

Talk to **leaders**, trade goods, host festivals. They leave after a few days.

### Rivals, raids & defense

Indigo camps share the frontier.

- **Diplomacy:** gifts, trade pacts, peace treaties, show strength, tribute / border events  
- **Raid them** or **counter-raid** when they march first  
- **Incoming raids:** defend with militia · barricade (wood+stone) · pay food tribute  
- **Prep matters:** walls, towers, barracks guards 🪖, stone/iron spears, shields, food stockpiles  
- **Map:** red dashed march lines; combat is **strength ratio**, not a separate battle screen  
- **Log → Combat** = after-action reports  

Research **Defense** techs (stone spears, fortification, iron gear). Iron needs forge + blacksmith.

### Magic, curses & spectacles

- **Moon Howlers** 🌝 — full moon ~every 2 weeks; cursed settlers hunt; Church may cure at dawn  
- **Taming Post** — befriend wolves/foxes/deer/rabbits (careful with Harmony goals)  
- **Renffr omen** — rare sky drama  
- **Map juice:** hunt chases, raid lines, night glow, build confetti, screen shake (toggle in ☰)  

### Victory paths & challenges

| Path | The brag |
|------|----------|
| **🌿 Eco-Utopia** | Large population **and** high ecosystem health for years |
| **🏰 Great City** | Huge pop + many finished buildings |
| **💰 Trade Empire** | All trade routes, many caravan trips, serious gold |
| **🐺 Harmony** | Live with **wild** (untamed) wolves + wildkin |

Mid-game **challenges** (Progress → Goals) nudge milestones without locking one style.  
Or ignore victories and play for the chronicle.

### Leadership & civic life

- **Town Hall** — taxes, immigration boosts, hosted festivals, elections  
- **Elections** — decades bring ceremony, gossip, new leaders  
- **Reputation** — unlocks trade, visitors, immigrants  
- **Log → Chronicle** — the story of your valley  

### UI you’ll use every session

| Place | What’s there |
|-------|----------------|
| **Header** | Resources, time, season/temp, speed, pause, save |
| **Left rail** | Build catalog by category |
| **Right tabs** | **V** Village · **F** Frontier · **N** Nature · **P** Progress · **L** Log · **M** More |
| **Map click** | Settler / building / camp inspectors |
| **Alerts** | Raids, trade ready, forge needs, food low… |
| **Minimap** | Jump around large maps |

## First hour — play this

1. **Intro** (skippable after logo) → name village, map size, land type.  
2. **House before first night (8pm).**  
3. **Farm** — builders show up; when done, farmers work 7–6 weekdays.  
4. **Watch people** — commute, chat, go home.  
5. **Nature tab** — don’t let ecosystem health freefall.  
6. **Lumber + food stock** before winter.  
7. When **visitors** arrive, click the camp and talk.  
8. Leave **some wolves** alive.

## How to install (alpha)

*Early alpha — Node required for now. Steam / installer planned later.*

**Need:** [Node.js 20+](https://nodejs.org) · Chrome / Firefox / Edge / Safari  

```bash
# repo root (folder that contains app/)
npm install
npm start
```

Open **http://localhost:5173**. Stop with `Ctrl+C`.

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| `npm` not found | Install Node, restart terminal |
| Blank / old graphics | Hard refresh `Ctrl+Shift+R` |
| Port in use | Close other servers; check terminal URL |
| Install fails | Delete `node_modules` in root and `app/`, reinstall |

ZIP download works the same if you don’t use Git.
