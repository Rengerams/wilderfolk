# PDF Studio — Professional PDF Editing, Straight From Your Own PC

**One application for everything you do with PDFs: edit, sign, stamp, split, scan, recognise and protect. No subscriptions. No cloud accounts. No files leaving your computer.**

*PDF Studio 0.2.0 · developed by Renger Vegting · contact: info@autosolid.nl*

---

## Why PDF Studio?

Most PDF tools fall into two camps: free viewers that can barely edit, and subscription suites that are expensive, heavy and push your documents into someone else's cloud. **PDF Studio is the third option** — a full desktop PDF editor that installs on your own Windows machine and does its work entirely on your hardware.

- **Everything in one place** — editing, signing, stamping, splitting, scanning and OCR no longer mean switching between four applications.
- **Your documents stay yours** — files are opened, edited and saved locally. Optional AES-256 encryption protects the local library with a passphrase only you know.
- **No subscriptions, no telemetry** — no accounts, no tracking, no uploads. The only network traffic you opt into is your own cloud endpoint.
- **Works offline** — the text-recognition engine and its language data ship with the installer, so OCR runs without an internet connection.

## What you can do

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Create & edit** | Start new documents (A4 to Tabloid, up to 200 pages), open PDF, PNG or JPEG, and edit text, highlights, rectangles, freehand drawings and pictures on any page — with full undo and redo. |
| **Organise pages** | Reorder pages by dragging, rotate, duplicate, insert blanks and delete — with live thumbnails of every page. |
| **Find & replace** | Search and replace text that is already inside a PDF, matching the page's paper and ink colours. |
| **Forms** | Read and fill the form fields of any PDF template, optionally flattening the values into a fixed copy. |
| **Sign & stamp** | Draw a signature with pen, mouse or finger — or type one in a natural handwriting style — then place it anywhere. Add professional stamps: approved, draft, confidential, paid and more, with your own wording, colour and tilt. |
| **Split documents** | Break a PDF into parts by page count, maximum file size or chosen page ranges — with progress while it works and every part named after the original. |
| **Scan & OCR** | Scan from a flatbed, sheet feeder or phone camera, then recognise text per page or whole document — review it line by line and keep it as an invisible, searchable layer. |
| **Full control of details** | Edit every document property, including every custom metadata tag, with the raw XMP packet shown for reference. |
| **Extract** | Copy any picture straight out of a PDF into an editable image object; export to PDF, PNG, JPEG or plain text. |
| **Print** | Print the whole document exactly as it saves — every mark in place, one sheet per page. |

## Who it is for

- **Offices and professionals** who sign, stamp and rework PDFs daily and are tired of per-month licences.
- **Anyone handling sensitive documents** — contracts, medical records, legal papers — where "it never leaves this PC" is not a feature but a requirement.
- **Archivists and privacy-minded users** who scan, OCR and store searchable documents on their own terms.
