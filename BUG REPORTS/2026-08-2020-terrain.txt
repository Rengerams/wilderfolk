 Bug Report: Landscape presets lack visual distinction and balanced composition

- **Status:** Rebalanced; pending live visual verification
- **Date discovered:** 2026-08-20
- **Version/build:** 0.6.1-line
- **Reporter:** Code and deterministic map audit
- **Area:** Truth | terrain generation | rendering quality
- **Severity:** High for presentation and first-session choice quality; no known simulation-authority violation
- **Owner:** Terrain generation authority

## Summary

The selectable landscape presets do not consistently look like distinct, intentional environments. Several presets are dominated by one terrain class, while Mountainous and Harsh overlap substantially. The issue reduces the meaning of the landscape choice and makes the generated maps appear noisy or visually unbalanced.

The current audited checkout defines **six** presets, not eight: Verdant, Mountainous, Coastal, Arid, Harsh, and Riverlands. `MapSetupScreen.tsx` renders `Object.values(MapPreset)`, so the live selector should also expose six choices in this source state. If another running build displays eight choices, that build must be identified before changing the authoritative enum.

## Reproduction

1. Start the audited Wilderfolk checkout.
2. Open the map setup screen.
3. Select each available landscape preset.
4. Use the same map size and seed where possible.
5. Generate maps and compare the dominant terrain masses, water coverage, mountain coverage, and forest distribution.

The deterministic audit used `MapSize.Medium` and seed `1234`.

## Observed behavior

| Preset | Deterministic observation before rebalance | Player-facing problem |
|---|---|---|
| Verdant | 7,302 dark-forest tiles out of 10,800 | Does not read as balanced grassland, forest, and rivers. |
| Mountainous | 5,712 mountain tiles, 2,631 rocky tiles, 893 snow tiles | Too much extreme high terrain and insufficient settlement space. |
| Coastal | 3,328 shallow-water, 2,415 beach, and 1,622 deep-water tiles | More than 80% of the map is water or beach; it reads as flooded rather than coastal. |
| Arid | 6,866 grassland tiles | Too uniform and lacks enough dry-land variation or landmarks. |
| Harsh | 3,741 mountain and 2,753 rocky tiles | Visually overlaps Mountainous and is dominated by grey terrain. |
| Riverlands | 4,630 dark-forest, 2,478 shallow-water, and 2,151 beach tiles | Reads as wet forest/coast rather than broad river valleys. |

The compact terrain previews also showed frequent single-tile type changes. The painted atlas can soften individual transitions, but it cannot create coherent macro-biomes if the authoritative terrain-type field is fragmented.

## Root cause

All six presets share the same terrain classification pipeline, but their `PRESET_MODIFIERS` values push elevation, moisture, water level, and forest thresholds too aggressively. The result is not a single rendering failure; it is an unbalanced parameterization problem in the authoritative terrain generator.

The river renderer had a separate `RiverBank` atlas-family bug, which has already been fixed in `src/game/terrainAtlas.ts`. That fix improves river continuity but does not by itself correct the broader landscape compositions.

## Attempted fix that was rejected

A macro-smoothing experiment classified terrain from smoothed elevation and moisture fields. It reduced some local noise but collapsed several presets into nearly one biome, particularly dark forest. The experiment was reverted completely. No unvalidated smoothing logic remains in the generator.

## Implemented fix

Adjusted only the authoritative `PRESET_MODIFIERS` table in `src/game/terrainGen.ts`:

- **Verdant:** reduced moisture pressure and raised the forest threshold to produce a broader grassland/forest mix.
- **Mountainous:** reduced extreme elevation pressure to create more foothills, hills, and settlement space.
- **Coastal:** reduced the water level and adjusted moisture so coast remains prominent without flooding most of the map.
- **Arid:** increased moisture range modestly while retaining a dry bias, allowing more terrain variation without turning it into a forest biome.
- **Harsh:** reduced extreme elevation pressure so it becomes mixed rugged terrain rather than a second Mountainous preset.
- **Riverlands:** reduced water and moisture pressure while retaining wet valleys, broad rivers, and river-adjacent terrain.

No new tick layer, simulation owner, or rendering authority was introduced.

## Acceptance criteria

This report can be closed after live verification confirms that:

1. Each preset has a clearly recognizable visual identity.
2. Verdant is not dominated by dark forest.
3. Coastal is not mostly water and beach.
4. Mountainous and Harsh are visibly different in both terrain composition and play space.
5. Arid has meaningful dry variation rather than an almost uniform grassland field.
6. Riverlands reads as broad river valleys instead of a flooded forest/coast.
7. The main canvas and minimap agree on the major terrain masses.
8. A fixed seed still produces deterministic terrain after the parameter changes.
