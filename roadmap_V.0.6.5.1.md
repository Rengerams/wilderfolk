## 0.6.5.1[19 october 2026]

- **Fixed:** synchronized the asset register with the shipped Village Square artwork (`square.jpg`) and updated the Square's sprite path; Graveyard and Villa are also marked as used by the building renderer.
- **Added:** automated Villa housing regression tests covering Better Housing unlocks, build-catalog registration, 14/16/18 resident capacities, the level-3 upgrade ceiling, and unchanged House/Mansion capacity rules.
- **Added:** Villa residence using `/sprites/villa.png` with shelter for 14 villagers; listed under Housing and included in residential occupancy and population housing capacity. Building a Villa requires the new **Better Housing** research in the Architecture tree (after Fine Construction), including on older saves. Villa upgrades follow the standard housing rules: level 1 shelters 14 people, level 2 shelters 16 and level 3 shelters 18.
- **Added:** a unique Village Square using `/sprites/square.jpg`, serving as the gathering place for festivals and elections, with the previous gathering locations retained when no completed square exists.
- **Changed:** the three leading election candidates stand at the center of the ceremony, election phases last longer (24 hours gathering, 20 hours discussion, 8 hours final tension), and each phase is recorded in the Chronicle.
- **Added:** buildable Graveyard using `/sprites/graveyard.png`, one Gravekeeper job per graveyard, and up to 50 memorial records per graveyard; names of villagers appear in green and enemy casualties in red. Adult relatives can occasionally visit recorded family graves outside working hours.
- **Added:** a single persistent **Weather visuals** switch under Graphics, controlling all weather overlays, particles, fog, clouds, storms and seasonal particles without changing simulated weather.
- **Added:** a paced full-screen text chronicle for outgoing raid battles, presenting successive phases and recorded battle outcomes and named casualties.
- **Changed:** victorious outgoing raids cause permanent rival population losses, with overwhelmingly successful attacks able to eliminate rival settlements and clear associated events and structures. Rival deaths are recorded individually where characters exist, alongside counts for off-map casualties.
- **Changed:** raid outcomes report spoils, own and enemy casualties, village victory announcements or mourning, and leadership rewards in the game Chronicle.
- **Fixed:** the simulation worker is only enabled in supported runtimes, so main-thread fallback and diagnostics work without a browser Worker.
- **Fixed:** prison guard patrols retain the assigned crew and do not leave their posts for social walks while commuting to a shift.
- **Fixed:** the Leader's House can be upgraded, with each level increasing the number of available beds.
- **Fixed:** valley ecology transitions continue to enter the Chronicle when warnings are muted, without bypassing the cooldown for visible alerts.
- **Changed:** continuous integration now typechecks and runs the full unit test suite, in addition to source checks and the production build.
- **Fixed:** raid chronicles distinguish threats, marches, combat outcomes, casualties and cancellations; raid totals and village traits no longer count the same raid multiple times.
- **Changed:** combat CSV exports include raid stage and kind, and the combat panel labels each stage.
- **Fixed:** removed the Gazette hook dependency warning while keeping live Chronicle updates visible; newspaper summaries are only calculated when the Gazette is open.
- **Fixed:** restored the CPU profiler integration removed by a later update, repairing the build and restoring start, stop, live samples and the final worker report.
- **Fixed:** the CPU profiler docks above the map's lower-right controls instead of covering the build menu; expanding its report no longer moves the toggle.
- **Fixed:** prison watch assignments keep the same weekly crew, including guards from overstaffed saves. Guards commute on weekends and off-screen; hourly post checks run after movement so arrivals count.
