## 2. Short blurb

Settle a wild valley where deer eat grass, wolves eat deer, and hunters share the trails. Build families, taverns, and forges—but if you wipe the wolves, the deer explode and the meadows die. *Wilderfolk* is a cozy frontier colony sim about living inside the food chain, not ruling it.

---

## 3. Long description

You didn’t conquer the wild. You moved in.

In *Wilderfolk*, your colony shares the trails with deer, wolves, hunters, and strangers under the moon. Grass feeds deer. Deer feed wolves. Your people hunt, house, argue, and elect a village head—while the valley itself shifts from Stable toward Collapse if you break the chain that keeps it alive.

This is a cozy frontier colony sim with real ecological stakes. Wipe the wolves and the deer explode; the meadows die; you lose to ecology as surely as to raids. Families form. Affairs happen. Taverns fill. Visitors arrive. Rivals watch. On the full moon, Moon Howlers remind you that the night still belongs to something older than your walls.

Build hotels for travelers. Paint the map of your growing town. Forge iron swords, scale mail, and tower ballistae when the blacksmith’s fire is hot enough. Immigration and housing shape who stays. Raids test what you’ve built. Days turn on a steady pulse of seventy-two ticks—time enough for a hunt, a vote, and a quiet walk home.

*Where Beasts and Kin Unite.* Don’t kill all the wolves.

**Early Alpha 0.5.0.** Colonies from 0.4 continue into the valley’s next chapter.

---

## 4. v0.5.0 What’s new

**The valley scales — kin, beasts, and forge-steel.**

- **Scale** — Bigger towns stay playable. The world breathes on spatial grids with smoother simulation and rendering so your colony can grow without the valley grinding to a halt.
- **Trust** — The sim plays fairer and clearer. Moon Howlers, housing and families, raids, immigration, and the hunt/day rhythm behave with more honesty so cause and effect feel readable.
- **Steel** — The blacksmith forge reaches tier 5. Craft iron swords, scale mail, and tower ballistae; arm your people with a clear armament checklist when the frontier bites back.
- **Life** — A hotel for visitors; valley stages from Stable toward Collapse; elections and a village head; a painted map; and days measured in seventy-two ticks—so community, time, and place feel lived-in.

**Saves:** 0.4 colonies can continue into 0.5.
