# 🌕 Moon Howler Nights — the exorcism overhaul

**Wilderfolk · v0.5.2+ · release notes for players**

The full moon used to be a coin flip from anywhere in the valley. Now it's a *night hunt* — and for the first time, **you can see it coming**.

---

## The short version

- Churches hold **up to 4 priests** — the more you staff, the better the odds (35% → **71%**)
- On full-moon nights, priests **leave home and actively hunt the Moon Howler**
- The exorcism only fires when a priest **closes in** on the howler — a howler that stays away survives the night
- **Barracks guards** can roll to save a priest from a failed rite (extra roll, not guaranteed)
- Active howlers show as a **pulsing red dot** on the minimap + a **red ring** on the map
- Howlers that slip indoors are **dragged back out** to hunt
- Kill a howler → earn the title **Moonslayer** · break a curse → earn **Howlerbane**

---

## What a full-moon night looks like now

**20:00** — the moon rises over a full-moon day. Cursed settlers transform and are pushed out of any building to hunt. The hunt is on.

**Meanwhile, your priests are at work.** If a church is staffed, its priests leave their houses on the exorcism shift and walk toward the howler. You'll see them moving on the map — and you'll see the howler, because it now carries a **pulsing red dot on the minimap** and a **red ring** under its 🌝 on the main map. No more scanning for the purple dot. The hunt is impossible to miss.

**The confrontation.** When a priest gets within range of the howler, the rite fires:
- **Success** → curse broken, the settler comes home human. The priest earns the title **Howlerbane** (shown after their name: *"Ingrid Howlerbane"*).
- **Fail, priest flees** → the howler stays cursed and keeps hunting.
- **Fail, priest killed** → the howler is still out there, and the surviving priests get scared — they retreat to the church before trying again.

**Guards matter now.** If a Barracks guard is nearby when the rite goes wrong, each guard rolls to **save the priest** — turn the kill into an escape. More guards nearby = better odds. It's a chance, not a promise: send your guards to the church on full-moon nights.

**Range is real.** The priest has to actually reach the howler (200px). A howler roaming far from the village can survive the night — that's the point. Exorcisms aren't guaranteed; every full moon is a *chance*.

**Weapons save lives.** The better your arms, the fewer settlers the howler kills on its hunt — shields give victims a block chance (wooden shields 35% → scale mail 72%), and iron spears/swords let them fight back (45%/55%). A settler who **kills** the howler in self-defense earns the title **Moonslayer**.

## The numbers

| What | Value |
|---|---|
| Church priest capacity | **4** (was 1) |
| Cure chance | 35% + **12% per extra priest**, capped at **78%** (4 priests → 71%) |
| Exorcism range | 200px — priest must reach the howler |
| Attempt cooldown | every **2 in-game hours** during the night window |
| Guard save range | 220px from the priest |
| Guard save roll | **50% per nearby guard** (extra roll, not guaranteed) |
| Night window | 20:00 → before 06:00 (the howler reverts at 06:00) |
| Titles | **Moonslayer** (killed a howler) · **Howlerbane** (broke a curse) |

## Tip

On full-moon nights: **watch the minimap for the red dot**, keep a staffed church, and put a Barracks guard or two near it. The priest hunts the howler down — your job is to make sure the priest survives to finish the job.

*Don't kill all the wolves. But if one's wearing your kin's face under the moon — well, someone's about to earn a title.* 🌙⛪
