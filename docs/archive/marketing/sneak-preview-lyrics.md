## Edits vs the original draft (surgical)

1. **"Wild was here we do not hurt we shape it" → "The wild was first — we don't break, we shape it"** — original stumbled over itself; this keeps the harmony-with-wild thesis and scans on the gallop.
2. **"Sven goes hunting bears have arms" → "Sven goes hunting — wolves have teeth"** — no bears in the wildlife roster (grass, rabbits, deer, foxes, wolves, wildkin), and wolves foreshadow the moon curse two sections early. *(Keep the bear line if bears get added — it's a fun line.)*
3. **"Send a gift or show the slap" → "Send a gift or draw the blade"** — matches the game's rival choices (tribute or raid) and cuts sharper.
4. **Punctuation tightened** across the gallop verses — commas as breath marks for the delivery.
