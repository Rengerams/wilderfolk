Night coming. *Wilderfolk* is a cozy frontier colony sim where you live *inside* the food chain — grass feeds deer, wolves hunt deer, your hunters want the same trails, and the valley is keeping score. Wipe out the wolves and the deer explode. You don't lose to a raid. You lose to ecology.
