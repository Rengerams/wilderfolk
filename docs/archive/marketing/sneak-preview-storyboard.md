## Production notes

- **Capture:** dev server + screen recording (or Playwright video). `scripts/playtest*.py` can drive deterministic sessions for scripted moments.
- **Speed is your editor:** 0.5× for drama (moon, founders), 1× for normal life, 2–3× for chases and the outro time-lapse. Speed persists in saves — set it per recording.
- **Zoom:** max zoom (5) for faces/eyes/axe; full out for the aerial choruses. Camera pans = click-to-focus on the target, or drag / right-drag pan.
- **Juice:** keep the ✨ Juice toggle ON for confetti, camera nudge, and night glow — it's the whole trailer.
- **Grade:** cool blue for night/moon, warm gold for festival/tavern/chorus. The game's season lerp + light pools do half the work.
- **Title cards:** only three — intro "WILDERFOLK", mid "Where Beasts and Kin Unite", outro logo + tagline. Let the footage carry the rest.
- **Approximations:** "door slamming / shutters", "face close-up half in light", and the "feast table" are the only shots without a 1:1 in-game equivalent — cover with light, shadow, and UI close-ups.
